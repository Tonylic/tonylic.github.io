Happy mail reader

Happy mail reader is part of a private assistant that runs on one household's own computer. It is not a public service: only members of that household use it, and nobody can sign up.

A household member who wants it approves read-only access to their own Gmail on Google's consent screen. The assistant then sends that person a short summary of the mail that needs their attention, and answers their own questions about their mail. It cannot send, delete, label or change any mail.
