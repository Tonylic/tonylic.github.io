Privacy policy: Happy mail reader

Last updated 16 October 2026.

Who runs it

Happy mail reader is run privately by one person for their own household, on the household's own computer. It is not a business, it shows no ads, and it has no public users.

What it can access

Only the Gmail account of a household member who has approved it on Google's consent screen, and only with read-only access (the gmail.readonly permission). It cannot send, delete, label, archive or change mail.

How the data is used

To write a short summary for that person of the mail that needs their attention, and to answer that person's own questions about their mail. The summary and the answers are sent only to that person.

Where the data goes

  • The Google sign-in token is stored on the household's own computer, in its system keychain.
  • To write a summary or an answer, the text of the relevant messages is sent to OpenAI's API for processing, with storage of the request turned off. OpenAI may keep requests for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. OpenAI does not use data sent to its API to train its models unless the account owner explicitly opts in to share it, and this account has not.
  • Records of the assistant's work stay on the household's own computer.
  • Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Happy mail reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

A person can end the reader's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/linkedapps (Access to your Google Account) by removing Happy mail reader. This takes effect immediately.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for this app.
